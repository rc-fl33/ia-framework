# EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation 2024/1689)

**Regulation:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council
**OJ Citation:** OJ L, 2024/1689, 12.7.2024
**CELEX:** 32024R1689
**Official URL:** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng

## Overview

The EU Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act) is a binding EU regulation establishing a
risk-based framework for artificial intelligence systems. Adopted on 13 June 2024, it is
the world's first comprehensive legal framework specifically governing AI systems.

The regulation applies a tiered risk approach: certain AI practices are outright prohibited;
AI systems in high-risk domains must meet mandatory requirements; other AI systems face
transparency obligations; and minimal-risk AI systems have no mandatory obligations.

## Scope

### Who is Subject

The regulation applies to:
- **Providers** who develop and place AI systems on the EU market, regardless of where they
  are established (extraterritorial scope for providers targeting EU users)
- **Deployers** (organizations and individuals using AI systems in professional contexts)
  within the EU
- **Importers** and **distributors** of AI systems in the EU supply chain
- **Authorized representatives** of non-EU providers

### Who is Excluded

- AI systems used exclusively for military, national security, or intelligence purposes
- AI systems used solely for pre-market research and development
- Natural persons using AI for purely personal, non-professional activity
- Open-source GPAI models (with exceptions for systemic risk models)

## Risk Tiers

| Tier | Description | Key Articles | Status |
|------|-------------|--------------|--------|
| Unacceptable Risk | Prohibited AI practices | Art 5 | Prohibited — applies 2025-02-02 |
| High Risk | Safety-critical and sensitive-domain AI | Art 6-27 | Mandatory requirements — applies 2026-08-02 |
| Limited Risk | Transparency-triggering AI | Art 50 | Disclosure obligations — applies 2026-08-02 |
| Minimal Risk | All other AI | None | No mandatory obligations |

## Key Articles

### Chapter II — Prohibited Practices (Art 5, applies 2025-02-02)
Article 5 prohibits AI practices posing unacceptable risk, including:
- AI that subliminally manipulates or exploits vulnerabilities
- Social scoring systems by public authorities
- Real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces (with narrow exceptions)
- Emotion recognition in workplace or educational settings
- Biometric categorisation inferring sensitive characteristics
- Untargeted scraping for facial recognition databases

### Chapter III — High-Risk AI Requirements (Art 6-27, applies 2026-08-02)
- **Art 6-7:** Classification rules — two-tier system (Annex I regulated products; Annex III use cases)
- **Art 8:** Compliance obligations
- **Art 9:** Risk management system (iterative, lifecycle-covering)
- **Art 10:** Data and data governance for training/validation/testing datasets
- **Art 11:** Technical documentation (Annex IV requirements)
- **Art 12:** Record-keeping/logging
- **Art 13:** Transparency and information to deployers (instructions for use)
- **Art 14:** Human oversight
- **Art 15:** Accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity
- **Art 16-21:** Provider obligations (QMS, documentation, corrective actions, cooperation)
- **Art 22-24:** Obligations of authorised representatives, importers, and distributors
- **Art 25:** Responsibilities along the AI value chain
- **Art 26:** Deployer obligations (instructions compliance, human oversight, monitoring)
- **Art 27:** Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA) for deployers

### Chapter IV — Transparency (Art 50, applies 2026-08-02)
- Disclosure when users interact with AI systems
- Machine-readable labelling of AI-generated synthetic content (deepfakes)
- Disclosure of emotion recognition and biometric categorisation to affected persons

### Chapter V — General-Purpose AI Models (Art 51-56, applies 2025-08-02)
- **Art 51:** Systemic risk classification threshold (>10^25 FLOPs)
- **Art 52:** AI Office notification and designation procedure
- **Art 53:** GPAI provider obligations (Annex XI documentation, copyright policy, training data summary)
- **Art 54:** Authorised representatives for non-EU GPAI providers
- **Art 55:** Additional obligations for systemic risk models (evaluations, adversarial testing, incident reporting, cybersecurity)
- **Art 56:** Codes of practice (compliance presumption if approved code adhered to)

### Penalties (Art 99, applies 2025-08-02)
- **7% turnover or EUR 35M** (whichever higher) for Art 5 prohibited practice violations
- **3% turnover or EUR 15M** for other violations including Art 50 transparency
- **1% turnover or EUR 7.5M** for misleading information to authorities

## Applicable Entities by Role

| Role | Key Obligations | Key Articles |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| Provider (EU or non-EU targeting EU) | QMS, technical documentation, conformity assessment, CE marking, registration, corrective actions | Art 16-21 |
| Deployer | Use per instructions, human oversight, FRIA, monitoring, incident reporting | Art 26-27 |
| Importer | Verify provider compliance before market placement | Art 23 |
| Distributor | Verify CE marking and provider/importer compliance | Art 24 |
| GPAI Provider | Technical documentation, copyright policy, training data summary | Art 53-55 |
