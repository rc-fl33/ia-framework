## Perspective & Voice Modes

**Define when to use different perspectives:**

### First Person ("I", "We")
**Use when:**
- Sharing personal experience or insights
- Building trust and connection
- Telling brand story
- Taking ownership or responsibility

**Example:** "I built this framework to solve a specific problem I encountered..."

### Second Person ("You")
**Use when:**
- Giving instructions or guidance
- Addressing reader directly
- Making content actionable
- Creating engagement

**Example:** "You can customize this template to match your brand..."

### Third Person (Objective)
**Use when:**
- Presenting factual information
- Discussing technical concepts
- Maintaining professional distance
- Academic or formal content

**Example:** "The framework provides a systematic approach to content creation..."

## Voice Review Questions

**Ask yourself when drafting:**

1. **Does this sound like our brand?**
   - If not, what's off?

2. **Would our audience recognize this as ours?**
   - What makes it distinctive?

3. **Are we being authentic or trying too hard?**
   - Does it feel forced?

4. **Is the tone appropriate for the content type?**
   - Technical doc vs. blog post difference?

5. **Are we delivering value while maintaining personality?**
   - Substance AND style?
